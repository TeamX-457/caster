The product

A system for how to learn, not just what to learn

CASTER turns study time into a structured loop: guided content, active practice, self-assessment, and visible progress.

1

Structured study guides

Subject and topic-based guides that break learning into clear, sequenced steps.

2

Active learning activities

Practice tasks designed to replace passive rereading with active recall.

3

Built-in assessments

Quizzes and assessments that show students exactly what they've mastered.

4

Progress you can see

Dashboards for students, teachers, and schools to track growth over time.
