The problem

Students are taught what to learn — rarely how.

Without a system for studying, revising, and self-assessing, even motivated students fall into the same traps.

📖

Passive reading

Rereading notes feels productive but rarely builds lasting recall.

🗂️

Weak revision

Cramming replaces spaced, structured review — so little survives past exam day.

❓

Limited self-assessment

Students rarely know what they don't know until it's too late.

📉

No progress tracking

Without data on their own learning, students can't tell if they're improving.
